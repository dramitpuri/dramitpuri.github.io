Leadership & Heritage

Dr. Amit Puri

Founder · Enterprise Cloud & AI Architect · Author · Advisor

With over 25 years of transformational leadership in enterprise architecture, cloud modernization, and artificial intelligence, Dr. Amit Puri established this Foundation on a simple yet profound conviction: AI is the most powerful amplifier of human capability ever built — and amplifiers only work on a signal that already exists. The Foundation exists to invest deliberately in that human signal across arts, medicine, education, and physical AI.

"We don't need fewer humans in the loop; we need deeper humans in the loop — equipped with rigorous judgment, artistic sensibility, and the technical fluency to steer autonomous systems."

Operating Axioms

Seven Guiding Principles

Across every grant, program, curriculum, and biomedical partnership, we hold unswervingly to seven core operating tenets.

01

Augment, Don't Replace

AI's mission is to amplify human capability, not stand in for it. Amplifiers require a live signal.

02

Preserve Productive Struggle

The cognitive friction that builds neural plasticity is a feature, not a bug to automate away.

03

Human Judgment Stays Central

AI can generate options and compute probabilities; the final call, discernment, and moral weight stay human.

04

Access & Equity Cut Both Ways

Arts education and advanced AI literacy must be universally democratized, not reserved for the privileged.

05

Interdisciplinary by Design

Breakthrough insights happen when classical art, biomedical science, physical engineering, and ethics share a table.

06

Honesty About Uncertainty

We report what the evidence shows plainly, identifying where hype outpaces clinical or pedagogical reality.

07

Lifelong & Iterative

Cognitive mastery and human ingenuity do not conclude with a degree; they are a lifelong, evolving practice.
